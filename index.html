Fika

A private, single-user personal assistant application.

What this application is.
Fika is a personal automation tool operated by, and for the exclusive use of, a single private individual (the account owner). It has no other users, no public sign-up, and no commercial purpose. It is not distributed, published, or offered to the public.

Why it exists.
The owner uses Fika to organise their own day-to-day life: reading and drafting their own email, managing their own calendar, tracking their own documents and photos, and keeping their own notes and records in one searchable place.

Google account access.
Fika connects to Google services only through Google's official APIs, with the owner's explicit authorisation, and only for the owner's own account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
